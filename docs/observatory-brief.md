# Who is involved in the volunteering KTP?

Research checked on 23 November 2026. These are project background notes for Common Hours.

## The partnership

The 36-month Knowledge Transfer Partnership is between **Nottingham Trent University (NTU)** and **TeamKinetic**. The work combines research into how volunteers and coordinators use platforms with software development, evaluation and knowledge transfer. TeamKinetic’s recruitment announcement names Professor Daniel King as the academic contact and identifies Innovate UK and TeamKinetic Limited as joint funders. [KTP project listing](https://iuk-ktp.org.uk/job/volunteer-management-data-scientist-software-engineer-ktp-associate/) · [TeamKinetic recruitment announcement](https://uk.linkedin.com/jobs/view/volunteer-management-data-scientist-software-engineer-ktp-associate-at-teamkinetic-4488790032)

NTU places the **VCSE Data and Insights National Observatory** in Nottingham Business School and identifies King as its lead. Its remit includes improving the quality and use of evidence available to voluntary, community and social enterprise organisations. The Observatory’s team directory lists **Diwali Pathak** as a Research Fellow at Nottingham Business School. These sources establish the academic connection. The recruitment pack sets out the exercise and its assessment criteria. [NTU Observatory overview](https://www.ntu.ac.uk/research/groups-and-centres/projects/vcse-data-and-insights-national-observatory) · [Observatory team directory](https://www.vcseobservatory.org.uk/about/)

## Questions for a future pilot

- **With volunteers:** Ask people to describe the same activity in their own words and identify which preferences they would relax. Use their answers to revise the vocabulary and test whether one-to-one support should be a preference or a requirement.
- **With coordinators:** Give coordinators records with a missing location or an unverified eligibility check. Observe which question they would ask, whom they would ask and how long verification takes. Use those observations to choose the minimum fields and the evidence needed to change a role from pending to confirmed.

These are proposed checks. The two fictional cases provide no estimate of volunteer retention, matching accuracy across the sector or coordinator time saved.
