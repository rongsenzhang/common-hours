# TeamKinetic and the UK volunteer-management market

Public-source review for the NTU and TeamKinetic KTP application · 20 November 2026

## Why this prototype

A volunteer asks for an activity they can do on Saturday in a step-free space. An organisation has advertised a relevant role and confirmed the day, but left the access information blank. What should the volunteer see, what should the coordinator check, and how could the team test their decision? This is the question behind the demo. TeamKinetic and the wider sector have already explored conversational discovery. The portfolio contribution is a small, reproducible way for a panel to examine how a recommendation is made, how a missing fact affects it, and what evidence should come next. [NTU vacancy](https://vacancies.ntu.ac.uk/Job/JobDetail?JobId=2699) · [ODI hackathon findings](https://theodi.org/news-and-events/blog/connecting-volunteers-what-we-learned-from-our-hackathon/)

## What TeamKinetic sells

TeamKinetic is a Manchester-based supplier of software for organisations that run or broker volunteering. A volunteer discovers and joins an opportunity, a provider describes the role and its sessions, and a coordinator manages recruitment, deployment, communication and reporting. Buyers include local authorities, volunteer centres, universities, charities, health services, cultural organisations and event organisers. The company's roots include a community application for Manchester City Council and work to share opportunities between the council and local universities. [Company history](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/our-story) · [Markets and purpose](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/purpose) · [Product](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/)

| England Hockey | The events manager used a live overview to spot staffing gaps in particular roles. | Can a coordinator distinguish a genuinely unfilled role from one whose information is incomplete? [England Hockey case](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/case-studies?casestudy=1274) |
| Willowbrook Hospice | Before adoption, rotas, hours and communications took substantial manual effort. | Does a suggested follow-up identify one answerable question, and how much staff time would checking it take? [Willowbrook case](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/case-studies?casestudy=1279) |

These are customer accounts selected and published by the supplier. They informed the questions above. Independent sessions with volunteers and coordinators would establish which questions matter most and where a proposed workflow causes friction.

## AI and interoperability already under way

- TeamKinetic dates its use of volunteer-feedback sentiment analysis to 2015. Its February 2025 release notes also describe an AI assistant that turns keywords into an editable opportunity description. Basic text generation is established product territory. [Company history](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/our-story) · [TeamKinetic 2.6.0 release](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/blog/2025/02/teamkinetic-2-6-0-major-release/)
- TeamKinetic and Do IT worked with the Open Data Institute on a DCMS-commissioned project to improve sharing of opportunity data. The published model distinguishes organisation, activity, role and session. Publication end belongs to the activity. Accessibility, requirements, skills and remote participation belong to the role. Time and place belong to the session. These distinctions give an engineer specific fields to compare during an integration review. [Standards project](https://standard.volunteeringdata.io/) · [Volunteering and Social Action Ontology](https://standard.volunteeringdata.io/ontology)
- In December 2025, TeamKinetic described Project Alpha, a winning hackathon prototype for conversational opportunity search using standardised data, an LLM and MCP. The ODI also records a separate team's work on gaps in published accessibility details. A useful follow-on experiment would test whether people can distinguish confirmed facts from missing information across a mix of providers. The cited evidence covers the hackathon. Deployment inside customer accounts would require separate verification. [TeamKinetic's account](https://teamkinetic.co.uk/blog/2025/12/conversation-triumphs-at-the-odi-volunteering-hackathon/) · [ODI organiser's account](https://theodi.org/news-and-events/blog/connecting-volunteers-what-we-learned-from-our-hackathon/)

## Alternatives a buyer or volunteer could consider

The comparison covers advertised workflows. Each row names the relevant overlap and cites the provider's material. It provides no comparative performance measurements.

| Organisation and audience | Advertised overlap | What it means for this application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plinth**, volunteer coordinators | Presents recommended volunteers for each role with skills, distance and DBS information. A person chooses whom to contact or shortlist. | Assisted matching and a human final decision already exist in the market. Interviewers can instead examine what this demo does when evidence is incomplete. [Plinth volunteering](https://www.plinth.org.uk/features/volunteering) |

[Volunteer app](https://volunteero.org/volunteer-app) · [AI support](https://volunteero.org/features/ai-volunteer-support) |
| **Access Assemble**, organisations managing programmes | Managers track applications by stage and see which need attention. The software also manages shift rotas. | Volunteer programme operations are an established purchase category. The portfolio can demonstrate a bounded experiment that fits an existing workflow. [Application management](https://help-assemble.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/14641819-manage-applications) · [Shift rotas](https://help-assemble.theaccessgroup.com/en/articles/14641055-shift-rotas) |
| **Better Impact**, volunteer programme administrators | Its UK brochure covers applications, online learning, shift scheduling and reporting of hours and outcomes. | Evaluation should specify whether an outcome is an appropriate recommendation, an application or an actual attendance. [Better Impact UK brochure](https://www.betterimpact.co.uk/hubfs/Brochures/software_brochure_uk.pdf) |
| **Reach Volunteering**, people seeking skills-based volunteering | Volunteers search by skills, availability and causes, then connect directly with organisations. Reach targets people with at least three years of relevant professional experience. | Discovery for one specialist audience is an adjacent service. Community volunteering may include first-timers with little formal experience. [Reach volunteer journey](https://reachvolunteering.org.uk/volunteer-your-skills-and-change-world) |

The DCMS-commissioned study of digital tools combined a market scan with 39 in-depth interviews conducted between August 2023 and February 2024. Respondents described the value of flexible discovery, the extra work caused by weak interoperability and concerns about loss of human interaction. Those findings justify exploring structured data and coordinator support. They do not establish demand, time savings or improved volunteer outcomes for this particular prototype. [DCMS research and methods](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-tools-that-support-volunteering/digital-tools-that-support-volunteering)
